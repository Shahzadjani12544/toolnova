About ToolNova

Welcome to ToolNova, a collection of simple, fast and free online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide useful online tools that are easy to use, accessible from different devices and helpful for everyday needs.

What We Offer

ToolNova currently provides tools for image compression, image resizing, QR code generation, password creation, percentage calculation, age calculation, word and character counting, unit conversion and PDF merging.

Simple and Easy to Use

We focus on keeping our tools simple. You should be able to open a tool, complete your task and get the result without unnecessary steps.

Our Goal

ToolNova will continue adding useful tools and improving the experience so that more people around the world can complete common online tasks quickly and easily.
