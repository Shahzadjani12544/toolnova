Free Image Compressor

ToolNova Image Compressor is a free online tool that helps you reduce the file size of images. It can be useful when you need smaller images for websites, social media, email attachments or online forms.

Select an image, choose your preferred compression quality and click the compress button. The compressed image can then be downloaded to your device.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Choose the compression quality using the slider.
  3. Click the Compress Image button.
  4. Review the result and download the compressed image.

Why Compress Images?

  • Reduce image file size.
  • Make website images easier to load.
  • Reduce the size of email attachments.
  • Save storage space.
  • Make images easier to upload to online services.

Image Compression Quality

A higher quality setting generally keeps more image detail but may produce a larger file. A lower quality setting can create a smaller file but may reduce image quality. Try different settings to find the best balance for your needs.
